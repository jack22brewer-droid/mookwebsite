—The work

From measurement to mooring.

Every project runs the same patient course — patterned on the boat, cut and sewn in our own loft, fitted on the water.

  • 01Measure & pattern. We come to your boat and pattern every panel to its exact lines and fittings.
  • 02Choose your canvas. Select cloth, color and clear-vinyl options from samples in your hands.
  • 03Cut & hand-stitch. Cut and sewn in-house on marine machines with UV-stable thread.
  • 04Fit & finish. Fitted on the water, seams tensioned, the final fit dialed in.

—Good to know
Do you really pattern on my boat?

Yes. For the best fit we pattern panels directly on your vessel. For some standard covers we can also work from detailed measurements you provide.

What canvas do you use?

Marine-grade, solution-dyed acrylics and coated cloths chosen for UV, mildew and salt resistance — sewn with UV-stable thread and corrosion-proof hardware.

How long does a project take?

Most custom covers and biminis run a few weeks from approved pattern to final fit, depending on the piece and season. We'll give you a clear timeline up front.

Are the fabric samples free?

Completely. Start a request above and we'll send curated swatches at no cost and no obligation.
