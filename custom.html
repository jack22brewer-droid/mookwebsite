Our craft

Every panel patterned by hand, stitched to last a decade at sea.

We don't pull covers off a shelf. A craftsperson measures your boat, patterns each panel to its lines, and runs every seam through marine machines with UV-stable thread — the way fine canvas has been made for generations.

  • Solution-dyed acrylic & coated marine canvas that resists fade, mildew and salt.
  • Double-stitched, weather-sealed seams with corrosion-proof hardware.
  • Patterned on your vessel — no generic sizing, no compromise on fit.

How it's made

From measurement to mooring in four steps.

  1. 01

    Measure & Pattern

    We come to your boat and pattern every panel to its exact lines and fittings.

  2. 02

    Choose Your Canvas

    Select cloth, color and clear-vinyl options from samples in your hands.

  3. 03

    Cut & Hand-Stitch

    Each piece is cut and sewn in-house on marine machines with UV-stable thread.

  4. 04

    Fit & Finish

    We fit it on the water, tension the seams, and dial in a flawless final fit.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you really pattern on my boat?

Yes. For the best fit we pattern panels directly on your vessel. For some standard covers we can also work from detailed measurements you provide.

What canvas do you use?

Marine-grade, solution-dyed acrylics and coated cloths chosen for UV, mildew and salt resistance — sewn with UV-stable thread and corrosion-proof hardware.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the piece and season, but most custom covers and biminis run a few weeks from approved pattern to final fit. We'll give you a clear timeline up front.

Are the fabric samples really free?

Completely. Request a set above and we'll mail curated swatches with no cost and no obligation.
