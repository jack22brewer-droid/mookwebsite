Who we are

A canvas house that supplies the trade and the dock.

Gulfstream began on the Gulf Coast supplying local canvas shops with cloth and hardware. Decades on, we still stock the materials the trade relies on — and we run our own loft, building bespoke covers for owners who want the real thing.

  • Marine-grade, solution-dyed canvas, clear vinyl and mesh — sourced and stocked.
  • Wholesale supply with real availability, fair volume pricing and trade terms.
  • Bespoke covers patterned on the boat and hand-finished, not pulled off a shelf.
