—Our process

From measurement to mooring.

Every project runs the same patient course — patterned on the boat, cut and sewn in our own loft, fitted on the water.

  • 01Measure & pattern. We come to your boat and pattern every panel to its exact lines and fittings.
  • 02Choose your canvas. Select cloth, color and clear-vinyl options from samples in your hands.
  • 03Cut & hand-stitch. Cut and sewn in-house on marine machines with UV-stable thread.
  • 04Fit & finish. Fitted on the water, seams tensioned, the final fit dialed in.
